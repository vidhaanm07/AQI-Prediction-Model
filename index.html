// 01 DATASET

Hourly urban air, 175K readings deep

Pollution and weather observations from monitoring stations.

Source

UrbanAirNet: Urban Air Quality and Weather Dataset on Kaggle.

Open on Kaggle ↗

Inputs & target

12 features: PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, SO₂, CO, O₃, temperature, humidity, pressure, wind speed and direction, rainfall. Target: AQI_Target.

Data quality

No missing values or duplicates, so no imputation was needed. Inputs were standardised with StandardScaler.

Key findings

  • Sensor and weather data predict AQI reliably (R² > 99.9%).
  • PM2.5 dominates, consistent with how AQI is computed.

Engineering value

  • Low-cost monitoring with just PM2.5, NO₂ and O₃ sensors.
  • Real-time alerts and smart-city dashboards.
  • Target traffic and industrial emissions.

Next up

  • Validate across cities and seasons.
  • Add lag features to move from nowcasting to forecasting.
